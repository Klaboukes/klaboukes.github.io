Left-handed

Play the guitar the other way round? One setting turns the chord charts and the scale neck round too, so they look like your own guitar.

Turn it on

  1. Tap the Settings tab.
  2. Turn on Left-handed.

Twangle remembers it. Turn it off the same way.

What changes

  • Chords: each chart is a mirror image, the lowest string on the right, as a left-handed guitar looks from the front. The dots, fingers, barres, o and × go with their strings.
  • Scales: the neck is flipped. Held upright, the thickest string and the fret numbers are on the right; on its side, the nut and the open strings are on the right.

The letters and numbers stay readable, and 5fr stays at the left of a chart, where every chart has it.

What stays the same

The tuner, the strum check, the 12th-fret check, the chord name, the pitch trail, the metronome and the capo table don't depend on which hand strums, so they look the same either way. With TalkBack, charts and the neck are read the same way too, from the thickest string to the thinnest.
