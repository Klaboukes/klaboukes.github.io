A calm, precise tuner. Free, with nothing to sign up for.

Play a string and Twangle shows the note and how far it is from being in tune, to a single cent. Then it helps you play: chord names, chord charts, scales, the capo and a metronome.

  • Every feature freeNo in-app purchases, no subscription.
  • No adsNot now, not later.
  • No accountOpen it and tune.
  • Nothing leaves your phoneTwangle has no permission to use the internet.

Tune any instrument

Guitar (6, 7, 8 or 12 strings), bass (4 or 5 strings), ukulele, 5-string banjo, violin, viola, cello and mandolin, in the common tunings or your own. The chromatic tuner does any other instrument, or your voice.

Twangle speaks with TalkBack, works with large text, and comes in 16 languages.
